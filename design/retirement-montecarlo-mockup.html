How it worksEach of the simulated futures draws a random real return for each asset class every year (lognormal, correlated as above), applies your contributions until retirement, then withdraws your spending less income, grossed up for tax, at the start of each year. A future “fails” if the money runs out before the plan age. Results are in today's money, so inflation is already stripped out. Estimates only, not financial advice.
